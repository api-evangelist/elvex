# elvex

> elvex is a secure enterprise AI platform that connects company data to leading AI models so teams can build agents and flows without code.

elvex is built for enterprise teams that want every employee — not just power users — to build, share, and govern AI work. It is model-agnostic (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Mistral, Llama, and custom models), integration-rich, and centrally governed with SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA compliance, SSO, role-based access controls, audit logs, and full usage analytics.

Key differentiators: multi-provider interoperability, a no-code agent and flow builder, a rich datasource and integration ecosystem, shared workspaces with an agent library, usage-based pricing, and "Flows" for reliable multi-step agentic workflows.
